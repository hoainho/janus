The Name: Janus

⚖

In Roman mythology, Janus is the god of beginnings, transitions, and doorways. He is depicted with two faces—one looking to the past, one to the future.

Like the god, JANUS evaluates both what was (baseline) and what is (current), ensuring smooth transitions between versions while maintaining quality standards.

Just as Janus guards the gates of Rome, our harness guards the gates of your codebase, preventing regressions from entering production.
